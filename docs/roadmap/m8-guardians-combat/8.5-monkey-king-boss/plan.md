# 8.5 Guardian 5: The Monkey King (Final Boss & Revelation)

**Goal:** Implement the climactic final boss battle against Guardian 5: The Monkey King, the truth-keeper of Sauraka Veil, followed by the revelation that the Five Veils were locks, not keys.

**Narrative Context:** [sakura_veil_story_and_characters.md#20-23](../../../sakura_veil_story_and_characters.md#L750-L848).

## Overview

- **Level:** 5 (Final Boss)
- **Veil Guarded:** Veil V (Life / Existence)
- **Role:** Final Guardian & Truth Keeper
- **Location:** The Pagoda Hill summit above the clouds

## Boss Mechanics

- **Phase 1: Martial Mastery**:
  - Staff attacks, acrobatic leaps, clone mirages.
- **Phase 2: Realm Manipulation**:
  - Manipulates spiritual energy, summons falling petals/stone pillars, ground shockwaves.
- **Phase 3: The Revelation (Mid-Fight Dialogue)**:
  - Halts combat as the Five Veils align:
    > *"You were never collecting the keys. They were locks."*
  - The spiritual barrier fractures as the true ending sequence initiates.

## Steps

1. [ ] Import and configure the Monkey King 3D model and animation mixer.
2. [ ] Implement multi-phase AI with dynamic distance management (melee staff combos vs ranged leap attacks).
3. [ ] Script cinematic dialogue camera transitions between phases using `View.js`.
4. [ ] Trigger the final revelation sequence, connecting to the M4 celebration / finale and unsealing event.

## Done when

- [ ] Multi-phase boss battle functions with smooth animations and fair hitboxes.
- [ ] Mid-fight dialogue cutscene delivers the revelation cleanly.
- [ ] Defeating or resolving the battle triggers the finale sequence and unlocks all portfolio achievements.
