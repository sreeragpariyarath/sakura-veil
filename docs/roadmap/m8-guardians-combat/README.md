# M8: Guardians & Combat System

Based on the canonical narrative design in [sakura_veil_story_and_characters.md](../../sakura_veil_story_and_characters.md):
Rei awakens in Sauraka Veil and is immediately confronted by an aggressive cursed beast—the **Shadow Werewolf**—before being rescued by the Nine-Tailed Kitsune **Kohaku**. To stabilize the realm, Rei must confront the **5 Level Guardians** protecting the **Five Veils (Relics/Locks)**.

## Progression

```text
Opening / Tutorial Encounter: Shadow Werewolf (Lycan Spirit)
      ↓
Level 1 Guardian: Agile Spirit Guardian (Veil I: Memory)
      ↓
Level 2 Guardian: Mountain Oni (Veil II: Identity)
      ↓
Level 3 Guardian: Buried Beast (Veil III: Emotion)
      ↓
Level 4 Guardian: [TBA - 4th Most Powerful Guardian] (Veil IV: Desire)
      ↓
Level 5 Guardian / Final Boss: The Monkey King (Veil V: Life)
```
