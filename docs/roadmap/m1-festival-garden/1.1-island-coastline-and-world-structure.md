# Phase 1: Island Coastline, Surrounding Ocean & World Structure

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved for Implementation  
**Reference Map:** `SAKURA VEIL: A Lost Soul's Journey` (Concept Map v2.0)  

---

## 1. Overview & Vision

Based on the canonical concept illustration, **Sauraka Veil** is a lush, supernatural Japanese fantasy island encircled by a vast turquoise ocean. Rei begins her journey reincarnating as a soul upon the southern sandy beach, encounters the Nine-Tailed Kitsune **Kohaku** at a sacred forest shrine, confronts the cursed **Shadow Werewolf** within a stormy dark clearing, and undertakes the trials of the **Five Guardians** culminating at the mountain pagoda summit.

This document establishes **Layer 1 (The Island & Sea Foundation)**:
* Converting the world from an infinite dry plane into an **organic island**.
* Surrounding the island with a **sunlit turquoise ocean and shoreline foam**.
* Fixing the **invisible wall issue** by placing physical barriers out in deep water beyond the beaches and cliffs.

---

## 2. Spatial Scale & Coordinate System ($400 \times 400\text{ m}$)

* **World Canvas:** $400 \times 400\text{ m}$ (centered at $X: 0, Z: 0$).
* **Island Footprint:** Organic oval/circle with radius $R \approx 150\text{--}170\text{ m}$ ($300\text{--}340\text{ m}$ across).
* **Deep Ocean:** From $R = 170\text{ m}$ out to the physical boundary at $R = 195\text{ m}$.
* **Height Scale ($Y$):**
  * Seabed: $Y = -2.5\text{ m}$
  * Ocean Water Surface: $Y = -0.3\text{ m}$
  * Awakening Beach / Coast: $Y = 0\text{ m}$
  * Interior Meadows & Groves: $Y = +1\text{ to }+4\text{ m}$
  * Sunken Torii Chasm: $Y = -2\text{ m}$ (trench)
  * Pagoda Mountain Summit: $Y = +25\text{ to }+30\text{ m}$

## 4. Phase 1 Implementation Details

### A. Island Coastline Math in `WorldLayout.js`
An organic island distance function $D(x, z)$ defined by:
$$R(\theta) = R_{\text{base}} + \Delta R \cdot \sum_i A_i \sin(f_i \theta + \phi_i)$$
* South ($Z > 0$): Lower, broader sandy beach shelf extending to $Z \approx +155\text{ m}$.
* East/West ($X \approx \pm 165\text{ m}$): Rocky coastal outcrops and coves.
* North ($Z < -165\text{ m}$): Sheer cliffline dropping into the northern sea.

### B. Terrain Splat Painting in `Terrain.js`
The $1024 \times 1024$ terrain data canvas paints:
1. **Interior ($r < R(\theta)$):**
   * Green channel ($G$): Lush grass and wildflower density.
   * Red channel ($R$): Stone pathways, plazas, and sandy beach shores.
2. **Coastline Transition ($R(\theta) - 10 < r < R(\theta)$):**
   * Smooth fade from grass into golden sand ($R \approx 180, G \approx 120, B \approx 0$).
   * Water channel begins ramping up to create wet sand and shoreline foam.
3. **Outer Ocean ($r \ge R(\theta)$):**
   * Blue channel ($B = 255$): Full water depth.
   * Green & Red = $0$.
   * Automatically drops floor geometry by $-2.5\text{ m}$ and engages the Three.js/TSL water shader across the entire perimeter.

### C. Rapier3D Boundary Wall Repositioning in `Floor.js`
* Four invisible boundary planes moved to $X, Z = \pm 195\text{ m}$.
* Height: $50\text{ m}$ (well above maximum flight ceiling).
* **Player Experience:** Rei can fly past the beach, over the surf, and into the deep turquoise ocean before coming to a stop against the edge of the world. Visual feedback is 100% intuitive.
