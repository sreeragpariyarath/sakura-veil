# M1: Festival Garden

The one map of the game, matching the concept image: a sunny sakura garden with a turquoise river, a red arched bridge, a torii gate, stone lanterns, and a pagoda on a hill. Small (about 80 × 80 m) and fully polished rather than large and unfinished.

The five festival lanterns from M2 get their final positions here, spread out so finding them takes the player on a tour of the whole garden.
