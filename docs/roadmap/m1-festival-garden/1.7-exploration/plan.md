# 1.7 Things to find between zones

**Goal:** the space between lanterns is fun to fly through, not empty.

## Steps
1. [ ] Flight lines: a row of tall sakura trees to weave through, the river to follow, an arch under the bridge to fly beneath.
2. [ ] Wind lines and falling petals stronger along the main routes, to hint at the way.
3. [ ] 2–3 small optional secrets (fox statue, wind chime that plays a note, hidden bench with a view); each gives a tiny reaction, no portfolio content.
4. [ ] Playtest: someone new finds all 5 lanterns in 5–10 minutes without getting lost.

## Done when
- [ ] No long stretch of flying where there is nothing to look at.
