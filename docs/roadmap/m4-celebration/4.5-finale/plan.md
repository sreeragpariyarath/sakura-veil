# 4.5 Finale and free roam

**Goal:** the 5th lantern ends the story in a joyful moment, then lets the player keep playing.

**Files:** `sources/Game/World/Lanterns.js`, `Fireworks.js`.

## Steps
1. [ ] On `finale`: big petal shower, fireworks sequence (~15 s), final line "The festival begins! Thank you for visiting." (M6.1).
2. [ ] Optional: a gentle camera turn toward the pagoda, without taking control away for long.
3. [ ] After the finale, fireworks keep popping every so often at night or on a lantern re-open.
4. [ ] Player keeps full control and can reopen any card.

## Done when
- [ ] The full loop (5 lanterns → finale) can be played from a fresh visit in under 10 minutes.
