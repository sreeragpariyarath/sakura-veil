# SAKURA VEIL

## Narrative & Character Design Document

**Project:** Sakura Veil\
**Document Type:** Canon Story / Characters / World / Boss Structure\
**Status:** Locked Foundation\
**Version:** 1.0\
**Primary Purpose:** Narrative-driven interactive portfolio experience\
**Engine/Tech Direction:** Existing Three.js/WebGPU browser project,
evolved into a game-like experience

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Project Overview

**Sakura Veil** is a narrative-driven 3D interactive portfolio presented
as a small exploration game.

Instead of showing the portfolio as a conventional website, the visitor
enters a mysterious world, explores its regions, meets characters,
completes objectives, discovers fragments of a hidden past, and
gradually reaches the truth behind the world.

The game experience is built around one central idea:

> **The portfolio is the world. The story is the interface.**

The player does not simply open a "Projects" page. They discover
projects, skills, experience, achievements, and other portfolio
information through locations, objects, characters, quests, memories,
and exploration.

The narrative should feel like a complete fantasy adventure while
remaining technically achievable with the available 3D assets.

## 2.1 Genre

-   Narrative exploration

# 3. The Central Story

## 3.1 The Premise

Long before the events of Sakura Veil, a young woman named **Kaori
Yamashita** lived an ordinary human life.

Something happened to Kaori.

She died.

However, Kaori does not remember the circumstances of her death.

Instead of moving on, she awakens in a strange supernatural realm called
**Sauraka Veil**.

She wakes with a different identity:

# Rei

Rei does not initially understand who she is, why she is there, or why
fragments of another life keep appearing in her mind.

Sauraka Veil is not simply another world.

It is a realm between life and death, a place where memories, spirits,
forgotten emotions, and unresolved identities can take physical form.

The world is beautiful, but something is wrong.

The spiritual balance of Sauraka Veil has been damaged.

Five ancient relics have disappeared from their sacred locations.

Without them, the world is becoming unstable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Kaori Yamashita

## Identity

**Name:** Kaori Yamashita\
**Role:** Rei's previous human identity\
**Species:** Human\
**Status:** Deceased\
**Narrative Role:** The mystery behind Rei's existence

Kaori is the person Rei used to be.

At the beginning of the story, Rei does not consciously understand this.

The player discovers Kaori's identity gradually through:

-   memories
-   visual fragments
-   environmental clues
-   dialogue
-   dream sequences
-   objects from her previous life
-   moments where Rei experiences unexplained familiarity

The story should never reveal everything immediately.

Kaori's past is one of the primary mysteries driving the experience.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Rei

## Character Profile

**Name:** Rei\
**Previous Identity:** Kaori Yamashita\
**Role:** Main protagonist\
**Type:** Spirit / reincarnated soul\
**Personality:** Curious, observant, determined, initially confused\
**Primary Goal:** Recover the five missing relics\
**Secondary Goal:** Understand who she is\
**Internal Conflict:** Is she Kaori, or has she become someone new?

Rei awakens in Sauraka Veil without a complete memory of her former
life.

She knows almost nothing about the world.

This makes her perspective useful for the player.

The player learns about Sauraka Veil at approximately the same pace as
Rei.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Rei's Character Arc

Rei's story has three major stages.

## Stage 1 --- Confusion

Rei believes she has simply awakened in an unfamiliar world.

She wants to understand:

-   Where she is
-   Why she is there
-   What happened to her
-   Why strange creatures exist
-   Why she occasionally remembers another life

At this stage, she is primarily reactive.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Stage 2 --- Purpose

After meeting Kohaku, Rei learns about the five missing relics.

She now has a reason to move forward.

She travels through Sauraka Veil and confronts the guardians protecting
the relics.

As she progresses, she becomes more confident.

She begins making decisions instead of simply reacting to events.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Stage 3 --- Identity

Near the end of the story, Rei learns the truth about herself.

She was Kaori.

But the story does not treat this as a simple answer.

The important question becomes:

> If Rei carries Kaori's memories, is Rei still Kaori?

The narrative should establish that Rei is not merely a replacement body
for Kaori.

She has created new experiences, relationships, and choices.

Her identity becomes her own.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Sauraka Veil

## 7.1 World Definition

**Sauraka Veil** is a supernatural realm between life and death.

It contains:

-   forests
-   shrines
-   mountains
-   forgotten ruins
-   underground regions
-   spiritual paths
-   abandoned settlements
-   sacred locations
-   corrupted areas
-   hidden memories

The world should feel inspired by Japanese fantasy aesthetics without
attempting to directly recreate real-world Japanese mythology.

Sauraka Veil is an original fictional setting.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. The World Has Two Layers

Sauraka Veil should visually communicate two opposing ideas.

## Beauty

-   Sakura trees
-   mist
-   moonlight
-   lanterns
-   quiet shrines
-   glowing flowers
-   water
-   mountains
-   peaceful spirit areas

## Distortion

-   corrupted forests
-   broken shrines
-   strange creatures
-   unnatural fog
-   damaged spiritual barriers
-   abandoned structures
-   dark underground areas
-   unstable memories

The contrast is important.

Sauraka Veil should be beautiful enough that the player wants to explore
it, while mysterious enough that they never completely trust it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Kohaku

## Character Profile

**Name:** Kohaku\
**Type:** Nine-Tailed Fox / Kitsune-like spirit\
**Role:** Companion, guide, mentor\
**Relationship:** Rei's first major ally\
**Narrative Function:** Introduces the world and gradually reveals the
truth

Kohaku is the first major character Rei meets after awakening.

He/she/they should feel ancient compared with Rei.

Kohaku understands Sauraka Veil.

However, Kohaku does not reveal everything.

This is intentional.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9.1 Kohaku's Personality

Kohaku is:

-   calm
-   intelligent
-   mysterious
-   observant
-   occasionally playful
-   protective
-   knowledgeable
-   emotionally restrained

Kohaku should not behave like a generic exposition character.

Instead of explaining the entire world, Kohaku gives Rei enough
information to move forward.

The player should constantly feel that Kohaku knows more than they are
saying.

# 11. The First Monster Encounter

Rei begins exploring.

She encounters an aggressive, shadowy creature:

# The Shadow Werewolf (Lycan Spirit)

It is a ferocious, cursed beast corrupted by the world's failing balance.

The werewolf attacks Rei with rapid claw swipes and brutal combos.

This becomes the player's introduction to:

-   movement
-   dodging
-   interaction
-   basic combat
-   health/damage
-   timing against multi-hit claw attacks

Rei is initially unable to handle the creature's aggression.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12. Kohaku's Arrival

Just before Rei is overwhelmed, Kohaku appears.

Kohaku defeats or drives away the creature.

The scene establishes an immediate difference in power.

Rei asks:

> "Who are you?"

Kohaku responds with something deliberately ambiguous.

Possible line:

> "Someone who has been waiting for you."

Rei does not understand.

Kohaku then explains that she is not where she is supposed to be.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 13. The Five Missing Relics

Kohaku explains that five ancient spiritual relics have disappeared.

Each relic once stabilized a major region of Sauraka Veil.

Without them:

-   spiritual corruption spreads
-   creatures become more aggressive
-   sacred areas lose their protection
-   memories become unstable
-   the boundary between worlds becomes weaker

Kohaku believes recovering the five relics may restore the balance.

Rei agrees to help.

This becomes the primary quest.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 14. The Five Relics

The five relics are collectively known as:

# The Five Veils

Each relic represents a fundamental aspect of existence.

They are connected to the spiritual structure of Sauraka Veil.

# 15. Guardian Structure

Each relic is protected by a major guardian.

The guardians are not simply monsters placed at the end of levels.

Each guardian represents a conflict connected to its relic.

The progression is:

``` text
Opening / Tutorial Encounter: Shadow Werewolf
      ↓
Level 1 Guardian: Agile Spirit Guardian (Veil I)
      ↓
Level 2 Guardian: Mountain Oni (Veil II)
      ↓
Level 3 Guardian: Buried Beast (Veil III)
      ↓
Level 4 Guardian: [TBA - 4th Most Powerful Guardian] (Veil IV)
      ↓
Level 5 Guardian / Final Boss: Monkey King (Veil V)
```

The five relics are recovered during the journey from the five level guardians.

The Monkey King is the final guardian and the character who reveals the
larger truth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 16. Tutorial Encounter --- The Shadow Werewolf

## Role

**Type:** Hostile Lycan Spirit\
**Position:** Early-game / awakening encounter\
**Purpose:** Tutorial enemy

The Shadow Werewolf is the first hostile entity Rei encounters upon awakening.

Equipped with rapid attack animations (left claw, combo strikes, right claw, back attack),
it puts immediate pressure on the player.

It teaches the player the basic combat and survival loop:

-   movement and spacing
-   targeting
-   dodge timing against multi-hit claw combos
-   basic spirit attack/interaction
-   health and damage mechanics

The encounter is intense but short: just before Rei is overwhelmed,
Kohaku intervenes to drive the beast away.

*(Note: Currently using the Skyrim Werewolf Rig as a development/prototype asset;
must be replaced with an original/CC0/CC-BY licensed werewolf model before public portfolio release).*

# 21. The Monkey King's Secret

When Rei finally reaches the Monkey King, she expects another guardian

He knows about Kaori.

He knows why she has been able to recover the relics.

He knows what the Five Veils really are.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 22. The Major Twist

Rei believes she has been collecting five missing keys.

The player believes the same thing.

But the truth is:

> **The five relics are not keys.**

They are:

# Locks.

The guardians were not simply protecting the relics.

They were protecting the seals.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 23. The Revelation Scene

The Monkey King looks at Rei.

Possible dialogue:

**Monkey King:**

> "You were never collecting the keys."

Rei:

> "Then what were they?"

The Monkey King:

> "Locks."

Silence.

Rei realizes what she has done.

She has spent the entire journey removing the seals.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 24. Why Rei Was Chosen

The story should not make Rei a random hero.

Her connection to the Five Veils is tied to her existence.

Because Rei is a soul between identities, she can interact with the
spiritual seals in a way ordinary beings cannot.

Her memories of Kaori also make her unusually sensitive to the Veils.

This is why the world responds to her.

This is also why Kohaku was waiting for her.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 25. Kohaku's Secret

Kohaku knew that Rei was capable of interacting with the Five Veils.

However, Kohaku did not initially understand the full consequence.

Kohaku believed restoring the relics would stabilize Sauraka Veil.

Later, Kohaku realizes that something else was happening.

This creates emotional conflict between Rei and Kohaku.

Rei may ask:

> "You knew I could do this."

Kohaku:

> "I knew you could open them."

Rei:

> "And you never asked what was behind them."

This moment should be quiet rather than melodramatic.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 26. Why the Seals Existed

The five seals were created to contain an ancient spiritual force.

The exact mythology should remain intentionally mysterious.

The story does not need a large collection of gods, demons, kingdoms, or
historical factions.

The purpose is simplicity.

The seals exist because something beneath Sauraka Veil must remain
contained.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 27. The Final Conflict

After the five Veils are restored, the barrier begins to fail.

The Monkey King confronts Rei.

The final guardian battle begins.

This is the most mechanically advanced fight in the game.

The Monkey King should combine:

-   fast movement
-   melee attacks
-   jumping
-   area attacks
-   defensive phases
-   environmental interactions
-   short cinematic transitions

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 28. The Monkey King Is the End

For the current version of Sakura Veil:

**There is no Dragon final boss.**

The Dragon concept has been removed from the project.

The Monkey King is the final major guardian and final boss.

This is an intentional scope decision.

It keeps the story focused and prevents the game from adding another
major character and another asset dependency.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 29. Final Ending Direction

After the Monkey King is defeated, Rei does not simply receive a victory
screen.

Instead, the world becomes quiet.

The Five Veils react.

Rei sees fragments of Kaori's life.

For the first time, the memories are not broken.

She sees enough to understand that Kaori died.

But she also understands that her current existence is not simply a
continuation of Kaori's old life.

She is Rei.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 30. The Central Message

The story should ultimately communicate:

> **Your past can shape you without completely defining you.**

Kaori is Rei's past.

Rei is not required to erase Kaori.

She carries the memories, emotions, and experiences of that life while
becoming someone new.

This gives the narrative an emotional conclusion without requiring
another villain.

# 33. Character Design Philosophy

Because the project currently does not have a dedicated 3D character
artist/modeler, the narrative must be designed around available
production assets.

This means:

-   Do not create unnecessary original 3D characters.
-   Do not add characters merely because the story needs another role.
-   Reuse world assets intelligently.
-   Use environmental storytelling when a physical character is not
    required.
-   Use dialogue, effects, silhouettes, statues, spirits, shadows, and
    objects to represent lore when appropriate.
-   Keep the core cast small.

The current core cast is intentionally compact.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 34. Asset Usage Rule

A downloaded 3D model is not automatically safe to use in a public
portfolio or commercial project.

Before integrating a model into Sakura Veil, record:

-   Model name
-   Original creator
-   Source URL
-   License
-   Commercial-use permission
-   Derivative-work permission
-   Attribution requirement
-   Whether it is an original asset or extracted/ripped from another
    game
-   Animation license
-   Texture license
-   Whether the model is recognizable copyrighted IP

## Important

A model that is labeled with a Creative Commons option on a marketplace
page should still be investigated if its description says it was:

-   ripped from a game
-   extracted from proprietary software
-   uploaded without permission
-   restricted to non-commercial use
-   copyrighted by another company

Changing the model's in-game name does not automatically remove the
underlying intellectual-property issue.

# 41. Dialogue Philosophy

Dialogue should be short and cinematic.

Avoid long paragraphs of exposition.

Use:

-   1--3 sentence exchanges
-   environmental pauses
-   visual storytelling
-   silence
-   character reactions

The player should discover the world rather than receive a lecture about
it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 42. Tone

The tone should combine:

-   mystery
-   melancholy
-   beauty
-   supernatural fantasy
-   quiet tension
-   occasional action
-   emotional discovery

Avoid making the story constantly dark.

Sauraka Veil should contain peaceful moments.

Kohaku should provide occasional lighter interactions.

The contrast makes the serious moments more effective.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 43. Visual Storytelling

Important information should not always be delivered through dialogue.

Examples:

### Broken Shrine

Instead of saying:

> "The shrine was destroyed."

Show:

-   broken torii
-   fallen lanterns
-   scattered offerings
-   corrupted flowers
-   fading spiritual particles

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Kaori Memory

Instead of explaining Kaori's entire past:

Show:

-   a hand
-   a photograph-like fragment
-   rain
-   footsteps
-   a familiar object
-   a voice

The player connects the pieces.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 44. Mystery Rules

The story should preserve several unanswered questions for most of the
game.

### Question 1

Who is Rei?

### Question 2

Who was Kaori?

### Question 3

How did Kaori die?

### Question 4

Why did Rei awaken in Sauraka Veil?

### Question 5

Why can Rei interact with the Five Veils?

### Question 6

Why was Kohaku waiting for her?

### Question 7

What are the Five Veils really sealing?

These questions should gradually converge toward the ending.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 45. What We Are NOT Adding

To keep the project achievable, the current canon intentionally
excludes:

-   Dragon final boss
-   additional final villain after the Monkey King
-   large pantheon of gods
-   multiple kingdoms
-   complicated political factions
-   dozens of named characters
-   giant RPG inventory system
-   huge skill trees
-   full MMORPG-style progression
-   custom character roster requiring a dedicated 3D artist

The project should remain focused.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 48. Final Design Principle

Sakura Veil should always prioritize this hierarchy:

``` text
STORY
  ↓
WORLD
  ↓
EXPLORATION
  ↓
PORTFOLIO
  ↓
GAMEPLAY
```

Gameplay should support the story.

The story should support exploration.

Exploration should naturally reveal the portfolio.

The portfolio remains the real-world purpose of the project.

The result should feel like a small, polished fantasy game that happens
to contain a developer portfolio rather than a normal portfolio website
with a game attached.
